ABYSSAL OCEAN SYSTEMS · FIELD OPERATIONS

NODE RECOVERY

Operate a deep-sea submersible across a descending ocean basin. Locate polymetallic nodules, inspect their metal composition and grade, then collect valuable samples before battery and hull limits force you to return.

Deeper water contains larger nodules and richer deposits — but pressure, darkness and distance from the surface increase the risk. Your cargo only earns money once it is recovered at the surface.

Game simulation: fictional resource grades, prices, depth and vehicle systems. Not real-world mineral survey or extraction data.

Steer with A/D, dive with S and rise with W. Approach a nodule and press SPACE to collect it. Cargo capacity is limited to 12 kg. Watch battery and hull integrity; deep pressure causes hull stress. Return to the surface to sell recovered cargo and upgrade your sub.
