Measurement (vs ideal scene)

PSNR and SSIM reward smoothness and can rank a blurry image above a sharp, noisy one. The effective-resolution figure is the first frequency where fused/ideal radial amplitude drops below 0.5: an approximate MTF50-style probe, unreliable on the bar scene.

Radial spectrum (Hann-windowed 2D FFT)

Cycles per 512 µm patch. Shaded region: beyond the native Nyquist limit, where energy is aliased or was never sampled.
